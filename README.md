# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × T × R) / 100

Where:
- P = Principal amount
- T = Time period in years
- R = Annual rate of interest

## Example

- Principal (P): 1000
- Rate (R): 5%
- Time (T): 2 years
- Simple Interest: 100

## Usage

Use the formula above to calculate simple interest for any given principal amount, interest rate, and time period.
